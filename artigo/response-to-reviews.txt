Reviewer#1, Concern #1:
The experimental setup needs more detail, including software, hardware, result calculation, numerical problems, and management of experimental bias and error.
Author response:
We expanded the experimental protocol to specify stream order, batch processing, fixed seeds, core parameter settings, replica-level outputs, and the summary statistics reported by the scripts. We clarify that the number of repetitions varies by experiment and that Experiments 5 and 6 use three runs. Counts are accumulated as integers, whereas Recall, Precision, and runtime measurements use floating-point calculations. No dedicated numerical-stability analysis or rounding-sensitivity test was conducted. Fixed seeds and repeated runs support reproducibility and describe run-to-run variation, but they do not eliminate possible record-order effects or uncontrolled background-load effects on runtime; these are now stated as limitations.
Author action:
We updated the Environment and Configuration subsection with the hardware and software environment (Linux Mint 22.3 Cinnamon, uv 0.12.7, and Python 3.11.5), row-order stream processing, consecutive fixed seeds, the experiment-specific number of repetitions, $q=2$, $R=116$, $R'=50$, $\theta=0.5$, and the block-capacity limit. We also clarified how counts, quality metrics, runtimes, and summary statistics are calculated, and identified numerical-stability testing and control of background system load as limitations. The reported confidence intervals describe run-to-run variability and are not presented as evidence of statistical significance.

Reviewer#2, Concern #2:

Author response:
We corrected the metric specification and recalculated the summary tables from the ten replica-level CSV outputs in the second results collection (V2). Precision is now defined as $TP/(TP+FP)$, and Recall as $TP/(TP+FN)$. Since the implementation does not enumerate the full Cartesian product of the datasets, a meaningful true-negative count is unavailable; the reported confusion-matrix components are therefore $TP$, $FP$, and $FN$.
Author action:
We updated the Experiments section with highlighted formulas for Recall and Precision, the definition $FN=|MP|-TP$, and an explanation that the candidate-pair count is $TP+FP$. A full numerical confusion matrix including $TN$ is not reported because true negatives are not enumerated.

Reviewer#3, Concern #1:
Clarify the rationale for the selected block capacities, eviction windows, and offset values.
Author response:
We clarified that the common block capacity was retained across policies for comparability and that geometric offsets were used as a sensitivity study over small and large processing batches, rather than as a claim of universal optimality. We also revised the different-offset analysis using three runs per paired offset configuration and report two-sided 95% confidence intervals. Since Offset A and Offset B were varied together, the results are interpreted as joint configurations rather than independent offset effects.
Author action:
