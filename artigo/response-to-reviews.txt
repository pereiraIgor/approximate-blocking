Reviewer#2, Concern #2:
Elaborate metric definitions and calculations, including precision, recall, and a full confusion matrix by dataset and method.
Author response:
We corrected the metric specification. Precision is now defined as $TP/(TP+FP)$, and Recall as $TP/(TP+FN)$. Since the implementation does not enumerate the full Cartesian product of the datasets, a meaningful true-negative count is unavailable; the reported confusion-matrix components are therefore $TP$, $FP$, and $FN$.
Author action:
We updated the Experiments section with highlighted formulas for Recall and Precision, the definition $FN=|MP|-TP$, and an explanation that the candidate-pair count is $TP+FP$. New numerical confusion matrices remain pending regenerated and validated outputs.
